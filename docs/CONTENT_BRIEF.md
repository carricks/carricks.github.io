# Content brief

Last updated: 22 August 2026

## Editorial objective

Present a multidisciplinary engineering career as a connected professional narrative, not as a chronological CV. Copy should show how electronics, robotics, automation, software, communications and energy systems reinforce one another.

## Editorial principles

- Write for technical decision-makers with limited time.
- Lead with context, contribution and engineering value.
- Prefer short paragraphs over role and date lists.
- Avoid numbered entries when numbering adds a CV-like feeling.
- Keep technical language specific but readable.
- Do not publish confidential architecture, operating data or client information.
- Every measurable claim must be accurate and approved.

## Publication policy

Company names and approved logos may appear in the Experience page. Project case studies remain anonymized unless explicit publication permission is documented. Current or former clients must not be inferred from an employer logo.

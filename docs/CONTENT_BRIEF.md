# Content brief

## Editorial principles

- Write for a technical decision-maker with limited time.
- Lead with the problem and the outcome, not a list of tools.
- Use short sentences, strong headings and proof points.
- Every claim must be specific enough to be meaningful but must not expose
  confidential project information.
- The public portfolio does not name current or former employers or clients.

## Information deliberately excluded

- Employer, client and project names.
- Non-public system architecture, operating data, event thresholds or results.
- Claims about a project's rank, scale or uniqueness unless they can be
  publicly sourced and approved for publication.
- A detailed explanation of a specific grid event. It can become a future
  article only after its facts, sources and relevance are reviewed.
